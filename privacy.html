Privacy

The join sheet asks for your email. City, and whether you’re interested as a drinker, a café, or a retail shop, are optional.

If you say you’re a café or shop, we also ask for the venue, plus a note if you want to leave one.

It goes through Formsubmit to hello@drinkmai.com. We use it to email you about the first drop and member pricing, and to reply if you asked about stocking Mai. We don’t sell your email. This site doesn’t run analytics or ad tracking.

Want off the list? Email hello@drinkmai.com and we’ll stop writing.
