Most energy drinks are loud.
Mai is deliberate.

Real matcha. Quiet bubbles. A pinch of sea salt. Steadiness, not spikes.

The people

Sam and Stephen met in college.

Caffeine usually did too much. The jitter, the crash. They wanted a can for a clear morning that still felt like themselves later.

They made Mai in a food lab in Denver. Lightly sparkling matcha, a light sweetness. The cans they kept reaching for had a pinch of sea salt. Brighter. Rounder. That’s the story.

The drink

Not an energy drink. Not a heavy latte.

Real matcha, a pinch of sea salt, quiet bubbles. The full ingredient list comes with the first drop.

  • Real matcha. The kind meant for focus, not flavoring in a loud can.
  • A pinch of sea salt. Softens the bitter edge, brightens the finish.
  • Quiet bubbles. Crisp enough to feel awake, soft enough to sip through work.
  • About 4g of sugar. Lightly sweetened. Not a sugar rush.
