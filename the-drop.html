What is Mai?

Lightly sparkling matcha with a pinch of real sea salt. Quiet bubbles, a light sweetness, calm energy.

Is it salty?

No. A pinch, to round the matcha and soften the bitter edge. Not a salty can.

Is it a sports drink?

No. The sea salt is for flavor. Mai is a sparkling matcha.

Is there caffeine?

Yes. It comes naturally from the matcha. No added caffeine, no energy-drink stack. No crash.

How sweet is it?

About 4g of sugar per can. Lightly sweetened, so it’s not unsweetened and not sugar-free.

How bubbly is it?

Low. Quiet bubbles that lift the matcha without turning it into soda.

What’s in it?

Real matcha, yuzu, and a pinch of sea salt. No synthetic caffeine. The full list comes with the first drop.

When does it launch?

No date yet. Final prototype round, and we’re looking for a co-packer. The waitlist hears first.

Where will it be sold?

Café-first in Denver, then specialty shops.

What does it cost?

Member pricing for the waitlist. We’ll share the can price when the first run is locked.
