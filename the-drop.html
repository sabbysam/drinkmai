FAQ

What is Mai?

Lightly sparkling matcha with a pinch of real sea salt. Calm energy, quiet bubbles, a light sweetness. The drink two college friends, Sam and Stephen, wanted when caffeine usually did too much.

Is it salty?

No. It’s a pinch, there to round the matcha and soften its bitter edge, not to make the can taste salty.

Is it a sports drink?

No. The sea salt is there for flavor, not for workouts or recovery. Mai is a sparkling matcha.

Is there caffeine?

Yes. It comes naturally from the matcha, with no added or synthetic caffeine and no energy-drink stack. Even energy, no crash.

How sweet is it?

About 4g of sugar per can. Lightly sweetened, so it’s not unsweetened and not sugar-free. Real sweetness, just a little.

How bubbly is it?

Low. Quiet bubbles that lift the matcha without turning it into soda.

What’s in it?

A short list built around real matcha and a pinch of sea salt. No synthetic caffeine. The full ingredient list comes with the first drop.

When does it launch?

We haven’t set a date. Right now we’re in our final prototype round and looking for a co-packer. The waitlist hears first.

Where will it be sold?

Café-first in Seattle, then specialty shops.

What does it cost?

Member pricing for the waitlist. We’ll share the can price when the first run is locked.
