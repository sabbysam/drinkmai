The pinch

Why the salt.

A pinch of sea salt does what it does in a good kitchen. It softens matcha’s bitter edge and lets the sweetness come forward. It’s there to round the flavor, not to make the can taste salty.

Matcha and salt already go together. In Japan, green tea salt is a seasoning for tempura and rice.

A pinch of sea salt

Rounds the matcha, softens the bitter edge, brightens the finish.
